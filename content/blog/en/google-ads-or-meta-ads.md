## Two different moments

Google Ads reaches people who are already searching, like “dentist near me” or “catering for 50 people”. They have a need right now.

Meta Ads reaches people while they scroll Facebook and Instagram. They weren’t looking for you, so the ad has to catch their eye and give them a reason to care.

## Start with Google Ads when

- People already search for your product or service by name
- The need is urgent, such as repairs, clinics or legal help
- Each customer is worth a lot, so a few good leads pay for the month

## Start with Meta Ads when

- Your product is visual: food, fashion, places, design
- People don’t search for it yet, but they buy it when they see it
- You want to build a list of followers, waitlist sign-ups or WhatsApp chats

## Most businesses end up using both

A common plan is to start with the channel that fits best, prove it brings customers, then add the other. Meta can bring new people in, and Google catches them later when they search for you by name.

> Whichever you choose, check your tracking first, so you can compare the two fairly.
