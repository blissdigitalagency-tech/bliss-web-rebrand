## Why this comes first

Google Ads and Meta Ads learn from the results you report back to them. If a WhatsApp chat, a call or a booking is never counted, the platform doesn’t know it happened. It keeps showing your ads to people who look like your clickers, not your customers.

Bad tracking also makes reports misleading. A campaign can look expensive when it is actually your best one, simply because its results go uncounted.

## The four checks

- Pick one goal. Sales, leads or bookings. Write down exactly what counts, for example “a WhatsApp chat that asks about prices”.
- Find where it happens. A thank-you page, a WhatsApp button, a phone number or a booking form.
- Make sure each one sends a signal. That means the Google tag or GA4 event, and the Meta Pixel with the Conversions API.
- Test it with one real action. Send the enquiry yourself, then confirm it shows up in Google Ads, Meta and GA4.

> If you can’t install tracking yourself, that’s fine. Tell your agency, and ask them to say clearly what they can and can’t see.

## What to do when something is missing

Fix the gaps before you raise the budget. A small campaign with clean tracking teaches you more than a big one running blind.

Once every result is counted, you can judge each ad by what it brings in, and move money to the ones that work.
