## What changed

Search used to mean ten blue links. Now many people read an AI answer first, and only some of them click through. If the AI can’t understand your website, it simply leaves you out.

Making your site easy to read for AI tools is often called GEO, short for generative engine optimisation. It builds on the same basics as SEO.

## Five changes that help

- State the basics plainly on the page: what you do, where you are, who you serve and your opening hours.
- Answer the real questions customers ask, in their own words, with a short direct answer first.
- Keep your Google Business Profile complete and matching your website.
- Add structured data so search engines can read your address, services and reviews.
- Make pages fast and readable on a phone. Slow, cluttered pages are harder for every tool to read.

## What not to do

Don’t write pages stuffed with keywords or made-up claims. AI tools compare sources, and vague or inconsistent pages are the first to be ignored.

> A simple test: ask an AI tool about your type of business in your area, and see what it says and which sources it uses.
