## Why look at everything together

Results rarely depend on the ads alone. A great ad sending people to a slow page, or a form that never reaches your inbox, will still look like a failing campaign.

So each review covers the whole path, from the first ad a customer sees to the moment they contact you.

## The five areas

- Ads: which campaigns and messages bring customers, and which to replace.
- Tracking: whether every result is still counted correctly.
- Website: speed, clarity and how easily visitors can contact you.
- Content: what people respond to on social media and in ads.
- Budget: where money is working, and where to move it.

## What you get at the end

A short summary in plain words: what happened, why we think it happened, and what we will change next. When a result is below target, we say so early and show the plan.
