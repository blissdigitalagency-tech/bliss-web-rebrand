## The challenge

Griya Asri’s lead form asked only for a name and number. The sales team spent most of the week calling people who were years away from buying.

## What we did

We rebuilt the website around each housing type, with clear prices and a short form that asks about budget and timeline.

Ads now optimise for qualified leads, not every form, and the sales team marks each lead as good or bad so the campaigns keep learning.

## The results

There are fewer leads than before, but far more of them book a site visit. The sales team now spends its time with real buyers.
