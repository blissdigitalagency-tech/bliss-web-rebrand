## The challenge

Harbour View was fully booked every weekend, but midweek occupancy sat well below target. Their old ads ran the same message every day of the week, so most of the budget went to weekend searches they would have won anyway.

The team’s first idea was a midweek discount. We wanted to test whether the right message could fill rooms first.

## What we did

We split the ads by day. From Sunday evening to Wednesday we targeted people searching for business stays, workations, and short breaks near the harbour.

We rebuilt the booking page so the midweek offer, the quiet rooms, and the fast Wi-Fi were the first things a visitor saw. Tracking was checked before any spend, so every booking was counted.

## The results

Within three months, midweek bookings from ads more than doubled, and the hotel never needed the discount. Weekend spend went down because we stopped paying for guests who were already coming.

The team now gets one clear report each month, with the next test already planned.
