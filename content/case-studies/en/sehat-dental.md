## The challenge

Sehat Dental’s previous agency reported thousands of clicks each month, but the front desk could not see where new patients came from. Nobody was counting bookings.

Without that number, there was no way to tell good ads from wasted spend.

## What we did

Before changing any ads, we set up tracking for WhatsApp chats, calls, and online bookings, and tested each one with a real enquiry.

Then we rebuilt the campaigns around treatments people search for urgently, such as toothache and broken teeth, and made sure the clinic shows up clearly on Google Maps and in AI answers.

## The results

For the first time, the clinic can see exactly which ads bring in patients. Spend moved to the treatments that fill the chair.
