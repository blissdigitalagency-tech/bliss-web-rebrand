## The challenge

Cendekia started advertising in June, when most parents had already chosen a school. Open days were half empty, and the last places were filled with expensive late campaigns.

## What we did

We moved the plan forward by four months. Early ads answered the questions parents ask first: curriculum, class size, and fees. Later ads invited them to an open day.

Every open-day registration was tracked, and the school team got a simple weekly update on how many families were coming.

## The results

Open days were fully booked, and the school reached its intake target six weeks before term.
