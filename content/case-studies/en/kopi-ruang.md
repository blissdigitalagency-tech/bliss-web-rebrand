## The challenge

Kopi Ruang posted beautiful photos and grew its following every month, but sales before 10 a.m. stayed flat. Most followers lived too far away to visit on a weekday.

The owner wanted to know which posts actually brought people through the door.

## What we did

We focused paid social on people who live or work within two kilometres of the shop, at the times they plan their commute.

We tested three messages: the new seasonal drink, the quiet place to work, and a loyalty card for regulars. The loyalty message won by a wide margin, so we kept it and replaced the others.

## The results

Morning sales grew steadily over eight weeks, and loyalty sign-ups gave the shop its own list of regular customers to message directly.
