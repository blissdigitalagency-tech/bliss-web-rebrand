## The challenge

Lumi’s launches depended on a single announcement. Sales spiked for a day and then dropped, leaving stock for the sale rack.

## What we did

We built a three-week launch: a preview for past customers, a waitlist, and launch-day ads to people who had already shown interest.

We tested short videos against photos. Videos of the clothes in movement won, so the budget followed them.

## The results

Most of the new collection sold in its first week, and the waitlist is now Lumi’s strongest sales channel.
