My site looks fine, but ChatGPT and Meta AI scored D. How?

Your page probably builds its content with JavaScript. Google runs that code, so Google sees the full page. An independent test by Vercel (December 2024) found that OpenAI’s and Meta’s crawlers do not run JavaScript, and no newer test has shown a change. Switch JavaScript off in your browser and reload the page. What stays on screen is what those two see.

I blocked Google-Extended to stay out of AI Overviews. Did it work?

No. Google states that Google-Extended does not affect inclusion or ranking in Google Search. AI Overviews runs on Googlebot instead. Blocking Google-Extended stops the Gemini app from using your pages. You stay in AI Overviews.

Do I need an llms.txt file, or schema markup?

Google says you do not need special files such as llms.txt, or special markup, to appear in its AI features. Schema markup still helps you get rich results in Google, so keep it. Neither one is required, so this tool does not grade either of them.

I got four A grades. Why is my brand still missing?

An A means nothing blocks you. It does not mean AI will pick you. An Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands (2025) found that mentions on other websites correlated with visibility in Google’s AI Overviews about three times as strongly as backlinks. Reviews, articles and directories all count.
